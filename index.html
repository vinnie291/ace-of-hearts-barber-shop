Frequently Asked Questions

♥
♥
♥

Everything you need to know before visiting Master Barber Vinny at Suite #28 inside Sola Salon Studios (University Mall).

Where is Ace of Hearts Barbershop located and where should I park? +

Ace of Hearts Barbershop is located at 10621 Braddock Rd J, Suite 28, Fairfax, VA 22032, on the 2nd floor inside Sola Salon Studios at University Mall (directly across Braddock Road from George Mason University). Free dedicated mall parking is located right next to or behind the Sunoco Gas Station.

How much does a haircut, beard trim, or straight razor shave cost? +

Our transparent Booksy menu pricing is: Male Haircut ($40 • 45 min), Haircut & Beard ($75 • 1h 30m), Kids 12yrs & Under ($35 • 50 min), Beard Trim ($35 • 30 min), Shape Up ($30 • 30 min), Hot Towel Straight Razor Shave ($50 • 40 min), Hot Towel Headshave ($50 • 40 min), Shampoo Add-On ($10 • 10 min), and Before / After Hours VIP Appointments ($100 • 1h).

What are the hours of operation for Ace of Hearts Barbershop? +

We are open Tuesday 9:00 AM – 7:00 PM, Thursday through Saturday 9:00 AM – 7:00 PM, and Sunday 9:00 AM – 6:00 PM. The studio is closed on Monday and Wednesday. Early-morning or late-evening Before / After Hours VIP slots can also be reserved on Booksy.

How do I book an appointment with Master Barber Vinny? +

Click any Book on Booksy button on this page to choose your exact service and time slot with instant confirmation, or call/text Master Barber Vinny directly at (703) 655-1109.

What makes Suite #28 different from a traditional walk-in barbershop? +

Instead of a noisy, rushed multi-chair shop, Suite #28 is a private 1-on-1 studio where Master Barber Vinny focuses exclusively on your cut. Enjoy tailored consultations for your head shape and hair texture, soothing hot towel and facial steamer shaves, complimentary Wi-Fi, and a pet-friendly atmosphere.

Is Ace of Hearts Barbershop close to George Mason University (GMU)? +

Yes — we are located inside University Mall right across Braddock Road from George Mason University’s Fairfax campus, making us a top choice for GMU students, faculty, and clients from Fairfax, Burke, Oakton, Annandale, and Vienna.
