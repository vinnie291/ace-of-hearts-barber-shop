Located inside Sola Salon Studios at University Mall (2nd Floor, Suite #28) in Fairfax, Virginia, Ace of Hearts Barbershop was built around a simple belief: when you look good, you feel good.

Every appointment with Master Barber Vinny begins with a personalized consultation tailored to your hair texture, crown growth, and head shape. Instead of crowded waiting rooms and rushed assembly-line cuts, you get dedicated one-on-one attention, sterilized instruments, hot eucalyptus towel rituals, and crisp straight-razor detailing.

01. Tailored

Custom scissor and clipper graduation shaped for clean grow-out.

02. Hot Towel

Steamed towel prep and straight-blade precision on every shave.

03. Private

Quiet boutique studio environment with zero rush and free parking.
